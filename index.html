Privacy Policy – Economy Nickname Sync Bot

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

Economy Nickname Sync Bot is a utility bot designed for use within a specific Discord server.

Its purpose is to synchronize eligible members' existing Roblox-based server nicknames with their UnbelievaBoat bank balances.

The bot only operates within its designated Discord server.

2. Information Accessed

To perform its functions, the bot accesses:

  • Discord User IDs: To identify individual server members.
  • Server Roles: To determine whether a member has the Citizenship role.
  • Server Nicknames: To preserve existing Roblox usernames when updating nicknames.
  • UnbelievaBoat Bank Balances: To retrieve the member's bank balance for display.

This information is accessed only as necessary for nickname synchronization.

3. How Information Is Used

The bot uses this information to:

  • Identify members with the Citizenship role.
  • Retrieve their UnbelievaBoat bank balances.
  • Append their bank balance to their existing server nickname.
  • Remove the balance suffix when a member loses the Citizenship role.
  • Restore the balance suffix when a member's nickname is changed by the server's verification system.

The bot does not deposit, withdraw, transfer, or otherwise modify economy balances.

4. Information Not Accessed

The bot is not designed to:

  • Read private messages or direct messages.
  • Read ordinary server message content.
  • Monitor members' online status or activity.
  • Access members' passwords or personal account credentials.

5. Data Storage and Sharing

Information is processed to provide the bot's nickname synchronization functionality.

The bot is not intended to sell or distribute members' information to third parties.

Discord and UnbelievaBoat process information through their respective services and APIs as necessary for the bot to operate.

6. Security

The bot's API credentials are intended to remain private and are not intended to be publicly distributed.

Reasonable precautions are taken to protect the bot's configuration and credentials from unauthorized access.

7. Contact

Questions or concerns about the bot or its handling of information can be directed to the administrators of the Discord server where the bot operates.

8. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated if the bot's functionality or information-handling practices change.
